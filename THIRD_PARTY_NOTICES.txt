The reaction data in data/deoxyfluorination.csv come from published
high-throughput deoxyfluorination experiments.

Measurements: Nielsen, M. K., Ahneman, D. T., Riera, O., and Doyle, A. G.
  (2018). Deoxyfluorination with sulfonyl fluorides: navigating reaction space
  with machine learning. Journal of the American Chemical Society, 140(15),
  5004-5008. https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.8b01523

File source: datasets/deoxyf.csv in the repository
  https://github.com/doyle-lab-ucla/bandit-optimization
  at commit 084bb561a200ee5950a215c7026012878a7bc065 (October 24, 2023), the
  code and data of Wang, J. Y. et al. (2024). Identifying general reaction
  conditions by bandit optimization. Nature, 626, 1025-1033.
  https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07021-y
